### Determinism concerns (for any tool) [inferred, with general facts reported]
- **BLAS / LAPACK threading**: threaded reductions change summation order, so results can differ in the last bits
  between runs. Pin `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`, `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1`, `MKL_NUM_THREADS=1` (MKL also has a conditional
  numerical reproducibility switch [reported]). The adapter should refuse to run if these are not set.
- **MPI reductions** (dot products, norms in iterative solvers) depend on process count and order: v1 should forbid
  MPI (single process) or pin the rank count into S.
- **Iterative solvers** stop at a tolerance: the answer depends on rtol/atol/preconditioner. v1 proposal: direct
  sparse solver only; the adapter independently recomputes the relative residual `||K u - f|| / ||f||`.
- **CPU instruction sets** (FMA, AVX widths) can change rounding across machines even with one thread. Bit-for-bit
  K3 can only be promised on the same container image digest AND CPU class; across machines K3 needs a calibrated
  tolerance. Which one applies is an owner decision (GOAL §13.8 "whether nondeterministic claims are allowed").
- **Hash coverage**: the installed library is outside `covered_modules` (see README cross-cutting finding). The
  adapter must pin and check the distribution identity itself.

## 4. Claim-scope vocabulary (config `chh-fem/1`, proposed)

Mirrors `chh-sim/1` rules: exact rational strings only, duplicate/unknown keys refused, every range registered.
- `simulator`, `version`, `image_digest` (pinned; any mismatch refused).
- `physics`: one of `elastic_1d_bar`, `beam_euler_bernoulli`, `plane_stress`, `plane_strain`, `heat_steady_1d`,
  `heat_steady_2d` (v1). Thermo-elastic coupling and transient heat: later versions.
- `geometry`: registered parametric shapes only (interval, rectangle, rectangle with circular hole, L-shape),
  dimensions with units.
- `mesh`: `{family: structured_tri | structured_quad | interval, n0: base divisions, ratio: 2, levels: 3}`; the
  adapter builds the nested meshes itself.
- `element`: e.g. `P1`, `P2`, `Q1` (registered list; each with its theoretical order).
- `material`: E, nu (or k for heat), alpha if thermal strain; each `{value, unit}`.
- `bcs` / `loads`: by named boundary of the registered geometry; types `fixed`, `roller`, `traction`, `temperature`,
  `flux`, `convection`, `body_force`, `heat_source`.
- `solver`: `direct` only (v1).
- `quantity`: from a registered list of **converging functionals** (section 6); `tolerance` with unit.
- **Units**: every dimensional value carries a unit string from a small registered table; the adapter converts to SI
  with exact rational factors and runs a dimension check before solving. Mixed systems (MPa with metres) are caught
  by dimension and magnitude checks, not trusted to the user [inferred].

## 5. Convergence evidence (K3)

Same structure as `sim`, adapted to spatial discretisation [inferred unless marked]:
- At least **3 nested refinements** (h, h/r, h/r^2, r = 2 proposed). Three is the minimum for an observed order
  [reported: Roache; Celik et al. 2008].
- **Observed order** `p_obs = ln(|q1 - q2| / |q2 - q3|) / ln r`; require `p_obs` within a registered band of the
  theoretical order `p_th` for that functional and element (e.g. `|p_obs - p_th| <= 0.5`?; UNCALIBRATED), unless the
  differences are at the rounding floor. This mirrors `sim`'s `MIN_RATIO = 2^(ORDER-1)` test.
- **Richardson estimate** `E = |q1 - q2| / (r^p - 1)` (with `p = p_th` or `p_obs`, owner decision) and the **Grid
  Convergence Index** `GCI = Fs * E` with safety factor `Fs = 1.25` for three or more grids and `3.0` for two
  [reported: Roache 1994/1998]. Require `GCI <= tol` and `|value - q_finest| + GCI + floor <= tol`.
- Re-execution reproduces every reported level value (bit-for-bit under the pinned image + CPU class, otherwise
  within the calibrated K3 tolerance, owner decision).
- Theoretical orders [reported, standard FEM theory, e.g. Hughes's textbook]: for smooth solutions and degree-p
  Lagrange elements, displacement error O(h^(p+1)) in L2, O(h^p) in energy norm; energy-type functionals converge at
  O(h^(2p)). Singular solutions (re-entrant corners, crack tips, point loads in 2D/3D) reduce or destroy these rates.

## 6. Registered metrics (proposed; unit, direction, range)

Direction only matters for future "meets spec" claims; v1 `computed_value` claims are two-sided.

| Metric id | Quantity | Unit | Direction (for spec claims) | Range check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fem_displacement_avg` | displacement averaged over a named boundary/patch (never a point value under a point load) | m | lower is better | finite; |q| <= registered fraction of the smallest dimension (small-strain validity) |
| `fem_reaction_total` | total reaction force on a named boundary | N (N/m in 2D) | n/a | finite |
| `fem_strain_energy` | total strain energy | J (J/m in 2D) | lower is better | >= 0 |
| `fem_stress_avg` | von Mises or normal stress averaged over a named region | Pa | lower is better | finite; >= 0 for von Mises |
| `fem_temperature_avg` / `fem_temperature_max` | temperature over region (max only for smooth problems) | K | lower is better | > 0 K |
| `fem_heat_flow` | heat flow through a named boundary | W (W/m in 2D) | n/a | finite |

Point stresses at re-entrant corners and point displacements under point loads are NOT registerable: they do not
converge [reported: standard FEM theory; inferred consequence].

## 7. K8 invariants (background laws)

Computed by the adapter from the solved fields, at the finest level [inferred]:
1. **Global equilibrium**: sum of reactions + applied loads = 0 (forces, and moments in 2D) within a relative bound.
2. **Clapeyron energy balance** (linear static): strain energy = 1/2 x work of external loads, within a bound.
3. **Thermal balance** (steady): heat in through boundaries + internal sources = heat out, within a bound.
4. **Temperature positivity**: all nodal temperatures > 0 K; inputs in Celsius are converted, never mixed.
5. **Maximum principle** for source-free steady conduction with Dirichlet data: temperatures within the boundary
   min/max. Caveat: the discrete maximum principle can fail for P2 or badly shaped meshes [reported], so this law is
   registered only for P1 on non-obtuse meshes [inferred].
6. **Symmetry**: for a geometry, load and BC set that is symmetric by construction, the mirrored functional values
   agree within a bound (the golden suite can check this; for user configs only if the config declares symmetry).
7. **Material bounds**: E > 0; -1 < nu < 1/2 (open; nu near 1/2 also flags locking risk for low-order elements);
   k > 0 [reported: thermodynamic/elastic stability bounds].
8. **Residual**: independently recomputed `||K u - f|| / ||f||` below a bound.
9. **Units / dimensions**: every input converted to SI; the claimed value's unit equals the metric's unit.
10. **Tolerance bound**: `tol <= MAX_REL_TOLERANCE * max(scale, |value|)` (`sim` uses 1/100; scale per metric).

## 8. Golden cases (closed forms) [formulas reported, standard engineering results]

- Axial bar, end load P: tip displacement `P L / (E A)`; 1D linear elements are exact at nodes.
- Cantilever (Euler-Bernoulli), end load P: tip deflection `P L^3 / (3 E I)`; Hermite cubic beam elements are nodally
  exact, so the convergence differences sit at the rounding floor (tests the floor path, as `sim`'s golden does).
- Simply supported beam, uniform load w: midspan deflection `5 w L^4 / (384 E I)`.
- 2D plane-stress cantilever: compare with the Timoshenko-Goodier elasticity solution, which adds a shear term to
  `P L^3 / (3 E I)` [reported; exact formula and its traction assumptions to be taken from the source by the
  builder, not from this scout].
- Patch test (Irons): a constant-strain field imposed on a distorted mesh is reproduced exactly [reported].
- 1D steady conduction, fixed end temperatures: linear profile, heat flow `k A (T0 - T1) / L`; with uniform source Q:
  `T(x) = linear + Q x (L - x) / (2 k)`.
- Composite wall: series thermal resistance `sum L_i / (k_i A)`.
- Constrained bar heated by ΔT (later, thermo-elastic version): stress `-E alpha ΔT`.
- Method of manufactured solutions for observed-order checks on 2D problems [reported: Roache; Salari & Knupp 2000
  Sandia report].

## 9. Fault-injection list (each must be rejected FOR THE RIGHT REASON, as in `sim._rejects`)

1. Unconverged mesh (pre-asymptotic levels) -> "unconverged" (observed order out of band / GCI > tol).
2. Functional at a singularity (point-load displacement, corner stress) -> refused at parse (unregistered quantity)
   and, if forced through a golden fixture, observed order out of band.
3. Units: E given in MPa with lengths in m (factor 1e6) -> dimension/magnitude check or golden mismatch.
4. Units: mm read as m -> same.
5. Wrong BCs: missing constraint (rigid-body mode) -> singular system detected; roller instead of clamp -> golden
   mismatch and equilibrium change.
6. Version drift: library version or image digest different from the pinned one -> refused.
7. Nondeterminism: threads > 1 or MPI enabled -> refused before solving; a non-reproduced level value -> "not
   reproduced".
8. Result record for another config or another mesh level -> "result is for config ...".
9. NaN / inf / float-encoded inputs; duplicate JSON keys; unknown keys -> refused.
10. Loose iterative/solver residual -> residual invariant fails.
11. Equilibrium violated (planted reaction record) -> K8 equilibrium.
12. Negative temperature in K, or Celsius passed as Kelvin -> K8 positivity / unit check.
13. nu = 1/2 or outside (-1, 1/2) -> refused.
14. Refinement mis-declared (levels not nested or ratio not as stated) -> refused by the adapter's own mesh builder.
15. Too loose tolerance -> "looser than".
16. Scope creep: statement not equal to the scope phrase -> K1.
17. (CalculiX only) hourglass mode with reduced integration -> energy/golden mismatch [inferred].

## 10. Tolerances (all UNCALIBRATED, owner decision)

Refinement ratio 2; at least 3 levels; observed-order band |p_obs - p_th| <= 0.5; GCI safety factor 1.25;
rounding floor 1e-9 relative; residual <= 1e-10 relative; equilibrium and energy balance <= 1e-8 relative;
`MAX_REL_TOLERANCE` 1/100 (as `sim`); DOF cap per level (e.g. 2e5) and wall-clock cap for K3.

## 11. Task suite (proposed families; LOFO)

- F1 beams and bars (1D): tip/midspan deflections, reactions; closed forms available.
- F2 plane-stress plates (rectangle, hole, L-shape): averaged stresses, strain energy, reactions.
- F3 steady heat conduction (1D/2D, composites, sources): averaged temperatures, heat flows.
- F4 (later version) thermo-elastic constrained parts.
- **Held out (LOFO): F3** in v1 (F4 once it exists) [inferred: different physics, so a harness edit tuned on
  structural tasks must not regress on it].
- Sources: closed forms from standard texts (cite formulas, do not copy tables, e.g. Roark's tables are
  copyrighted [reported]); MMS-generated problems (no licence issue); NAFEMS benchmarks are paid/copyrighted
  publications [reported]: owner decision.
- Task design caveat [inferred]: closed-form answers are public knowledge, so a task "compute q" tests nothing about
  the research loop. Tasks should be design or search tasks (e.g. smallest section with averaged stress below a
  limit) where the certificate comes from re-execution and the scoring truth is minted after the freeze (§16.2).
